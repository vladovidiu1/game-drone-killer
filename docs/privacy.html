Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Drone Killer: Frontline (“the Game”) handles information. The Game is operated by Pintilie Vlad Ovidiu (“we”, “us”). Contact: vlad.ovidiu.pintilie@gmail.com.

1. Game progress and local information

The Game stores campaign progress, settings, achievements, weapon upgrades and in-game currency on your device to provide gameplay and preserve progress. This information is stored locally by the Game. Removing the Game or clearing its data may delete local progress. The Game can be played offline; advertising features require network access.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

The Game includes a Google AdMob advertising integration for optional rewarded ads. When advertising is enabled, choosing to view an ad may cause Google and its advertising partners to process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic data, and consent choices. The precise data and purposes depend on Google’s SDK, your region, and your privacy selections. We do not control Google's own retention practices.

Watching an ad is optional. The Game should grant its in-game reward only after an eligible earned-reward event. You can continue playing without watching ads. Advertising may not be available in all circumstances.

For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and your choices

Where required, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices before requesting ads. Depending on your location and the choices offered, you may accept, reject, or manage advertising consent. Where applicable, you can revisit privacy choices using the Game's privacy options control. If an applicable consent process cannot be completed, advertising may be unavailable; core offline gameplay remains available.

You can also manage Android advertising and privacy controls in your device settings. If you want to exercise privacy rights regarding information controlled by us, contact the email above. For information processed independently by Google, consult Google's privacy resources.

4. Purpose and legal basis

We use local gameplay data to provide the Game and remember your settings and progress. Where consent is required for advertising-related processing, the applicable consent mechanism is used. Other processing by advertising providers may rely on legal bases explained in their own disclosures. Availability and lawful basis vary by jurisdiction and processing purpose.

5. Sharing and third parties

Local game-progress data is not intentionally transmitted to our own server as part of the described game-save functionality. When the advertising SDK is enabled, Google and its partners may receive and process information necessary for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement and SDK operation, subject to their policies and your choices. We do not sell local game saves to advertisers.

Locally stored gameplay data typically remains on your device until removed by you or the operating system. We do not set the retention period for data processed by Google or its partners. We take reasonable steps in the Game's design to avoid unnecessary collection, but no technology is entirely risk-free.
