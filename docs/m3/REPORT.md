# Measured M3 economy report

Generated by `python tools/report_m3_economy.py`. Raw [campaign results](campaign_balance.json), [accounting scenarios](economy_scenarios.json) and [fixed-policy comparison](fixed_policy/balance.json) are retained alongside this report.

First-pass War Credits: **12520 → 8640** (31.0% lower), including objectives. First-clear mission rewards: 8500; once-only objectives: 140. Replay victories give floor(first-clear × 15%).

Connected assisted campaign: **25/25 victories**, no injected credits, ads or preliminary grinding. After an actual undefended defeat at 25, missions 24 and 25 were reconquered for **84 + 87 WC**, preserving research. Total purchases over these 28 deployments: **7815 WC**; ending bank: **996 WC**.

The reproducible pilot buys basic research/unlocks through the real purchase API, builds the existing MG/Cannon/Missile layout, prioritizes real threats, switches Standard/AP for the visible target, and may spend its actual BC on repair, EMP and overclock. Aim is deterministic assisted input, not gameplay auto-aim. Ammunition switches pay the ordinary cooldown and never refill. It does not prove a human win rate or Android handling. This policy differs from M2; income curves are directly comparable, combat outcomes are not a controlled before/after skill comparison.

## Observed tuning issue and correction

The first candidate used Scout/Assault/Heavy bounties 6/12/35 and a 0.75 construction-cost multiplier for temporary upgrades. An earned-budget run failed at mission 18. Changing the pilot to an EMP tower in every second slot also failed earlier, at 11: support cannot replace damage everywhere. The final candidate uses 7/13/40 bounties and preserves the existing 0.65 multiplier. A target-aware Standard/AP policy and paid tactical abilities establish the reported no-ad path. These refinements retain pressure without mandatory farming. See [ECONOMY_BALANCE.md](../../ECONOMY_BALANCE.md) for player-facing guidance and remaining human testing.
