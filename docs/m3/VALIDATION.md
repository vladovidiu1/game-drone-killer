## Tuning findings

[Recorded failed candidates](tuning_failures.json) preserve the initial mission-18 economic bottleneck. The first candidate reduced Scout/Assault/Heavy bounties to 6/12/35 and raised temporary upgrade prices to 0.75 × base cost × level; it failed at 18. Retaining 0.65 upgrade pricing and using 7/13/40 improved survival but the old AP-only/no-offensive-abilities policy still failed at 18. A separate EMP-tower-everywhere experiment failed at 11. The final connected pilot uses ordinary target-appropriate Standard/AP selection and paid EMP/overclock support; it completes the entire campaign with its own income. There are no reduced enemy stats, forced survivors, free abilities or injected victory events in that run.

Unit tests with obsolete old bounty/reward assertions were updated to the intended new integer values; scheduling/collision/ownership assertions remain. Funded research/art fixtures are separate from no-ad progression. Assertions were not disabled to obtain passing results.

## Remaining validation

No physical Android device, human spending study, human win-rate/difficulty test, audible hardware check or thermal/frame-rate measurement was performed. Native injected touch checks do not prove physical multitouch/cutout behavior. The deterministic pilot has exact timing and assisted aim, so these results demonstrate a viable offline path and catch accounting regressions; they do not establish final human difficulty.

Recommended human playtests: first-time mission 1 with MG+Cannon or Missile+MG; saving for AP versus two early upgrades; midgame choices among new coverage, upgrades and emergency reserve; returning after several losses; late missions with a focused level-3/4 build. Track spending, abandoned builds, repair use and retries before adjusting the next balance iteration. Existing visual-asset approval and Android optimization limitations remain in the graphics audit.
