## Remaining verification

No physical Android device, Android export/APK, touch latency, cutout hardware, audible cue, thermal/battery behavior or representative-device FPS was measured. Injected Linux multitouch is not hardware validation. Human testing should compare spread/concentrated defenses, rear blind cones and rotating bearings, moving during late radar warnings, cancellation under pressure and support placement; broaden seed samples before declaring final difficulty. Existing generated artwork still needs the art review documented in the graphics audit. M6 adds utility overlays and reuses existing raised-deck art; no new external illustration is required, and no production-readiness claim is made.
